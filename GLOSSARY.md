# Chan Kar Yik Portfolio

The personal website of Chan Kar Yik, a frontend developer. It is a calling card: anyone who has just heard the name (a recruiter, a Prospect, a peer) learns who Chan Kar Yik is, what they have shipped, and how to reach them.

## Language

### The site

**Headline**:
The single short line under Chan Kar Yik's name that states their craft and experience. It names no employer and no seniority level.
_Avoid_: title, tagline, job title

**Prospect**:
A person or organization who might hire Chan Kar Yik for freelance work.
_Avoid_: client, lead

**Smiley**:
The site's own logo: a smiley face that stands for Chan Kar Yik. Logos of other brands, such as Vue or GitHub, are brand logos.
_Avoid_: logo, mark, avatar

**Resume**:
The PDF of Chan Kar Yik's profile that visitors download from the site.
_Avoid_: CV, résumé

**Post**:
A single piece of writing published on the blog.
_Avoid_: article, entry

### Work

**Project**:
A shipped piece of work built for a Client.
_Avoid_: work, case study, portfolio item

**Selected Project**:
One of about six Projects shown in full on the home page. Every other Project is listed on a single line.
_Avoid_: featured project, highlight

**Client**:
The organization a Project was built for.
_Avoid_: customer, brand

**Agency**:
The company a Project was delivered through, usually Chan Kar Yik's employer at the time.
_Avoid_: vendor, studio

**Freelance**:
Work Chan Kar Yik did directly for a Client, with no Agency in between.
_Avoid_: self-employed, contract work

### Career

**Role**:
A position Chan Kar Yik held at one company for a period, such as Frontend Lead at Bitlab Digital.
_Avoid_: position, job, experience
