Joking, mostly. I wrote every line by hand for seven years, then started working with AI agents in 2025. The agent brings the syntax and a second opinion. I bring the judgment: what to build, what to cut, what will break. Together we weigh more options, catch more edge cases and make better calls than I would alone. Now I'm the one at work asking, “Have you tried an agent for that?”

**The agent knows Rust. I know what good software looks like.**
